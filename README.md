## Portfolio Projects

### 1. Employee Leave Management | Business Analysis, SQL & Power BI

A business analysis and reporting case study focused on improving visibility into employee leave requests, approval status, leave balances, and management reporting.

**Key Highlights**
- Defined functional requirements, stakeholders, and business rules.
- Used SQL to investigate leave requests, validate data, and identify exceptions.
- Developed a Power BI dashboard and data model to support management reporting.
- Documented business insights and areas requiring further investigation.

**Tools:** SQL, SQLite, Power BI, DAX, Business Analysis

[View Employee Leave Management Project](projects/employee-leave-management/README.md)

### 2. Fleet Maintenance | Work Order & Approval Tracker

A simulated business analysis and automation project focused on tracking fleet maintenance activities, identifying overdue work orders, and monitoring pending approvals.

**Key Highlights**
- Created an Excel-based work-order tracker and operational dashboard.
- Applied Power Query transformations to prepare data for reporting.
- Developed and tested a Power Automate Desktop workflow for overdue and approval monitoring.
- Prepared a Business Requirements Document (BRD), business logic guide, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) scenarios.
- Validated the automation using 10 sample work orders, identifying 7 overdue items and 1 pending approval.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Automate Desktop, Business Analysis, UAT
